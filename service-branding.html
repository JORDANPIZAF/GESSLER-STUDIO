Brand foundation

We build brand identities that feel coherent across every touchpoint.

This service is designed for businesses that need more than a logo. We define the visual language, structure and direction that help a brand appear consistent, professional and easier to recognize.

Branding can include new identities, refinements of existing systems or clearer guidance for how the brand should behave across digital and printed materials.

Best fit for
  • New businesses
  • Rebrands
  • Growing studios
  • Brands that need consistency

Identity direction

  • Visual concept and mood direction
  • Logo system and variations
